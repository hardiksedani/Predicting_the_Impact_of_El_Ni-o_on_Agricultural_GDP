C. Harmonization and Quality Control 
Annual rainfall is the sum of available valid daily 
observations in the financial year. Daily mean temperature is 
calculated from the maximum and minimum temperatures, 
and those daily means are averaged over the year. A 95% 
daily-coverage criterion screens  the climate aggregates. The 
retained row records rainfall coverage, temperature coverage, 
expected days, and the selected cells. This design makes 
missingness visible rather than hiding it behind an annual 
number.

across an entire state, particularly where elevation, coastlines, 
or rainfall gradients are pronounced.

IV. METHODOLOGY 
A. Feature Representation and Chronological Split 
Each observation contains rainfall, mean temperature, 
ONI, and a one -hot state vector. The final input has 31 
columns: three climate variables and 28 state indicators. The 
year is used to construct the split but is not itself a predictive 
feature. All states present in the test set occur in training. The 
first ten financial years, 2011 –12 through 2020 –21, supply 
280 training rows. The final four years, 2021 –22 through 
2024–25, supply 105 test rows. No random redistribution of 
state-years is used for the reported comparison.

The split uses observed climate from the held -out years. 
It therefore evaluates retrospective estimation conditional on 
realized weather, not an operational forecast issued before 
those years begin. A usable advance forecast would require 
weather and ENSO inputs available at the issue date, together 
with a treatment of their uncertainty. This distinction prevents 
a chronological split from being mistaken for proof that the 
full information set was available in advance.
